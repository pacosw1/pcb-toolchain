## Diagnosis (hand NET-CORE placement, v8 = 1592.9 mm / 285 vias against 1495.3 mm / 280 for v4)

Per-net length v4 (committed board) against v8 (`netlen.py`): the sum of the excess is 115 mm on 64 nets, 18 mm
shorter on 14 nets. Top: /ETH_TXD0 +21.2, /ETH_TXD1 +16.9, /PHY_RBIAS +10.4, VBUS +7.3, VDD_CORE +6.7, /UART0_RX +5.5,
VCC_DRAM +5.3, /ETH_TX_EN +5.1, /ETH_RXD1 +4.0.

New debug tool `IOTROUTE_LENDIAG=all|net,net`: per net the A* objective of its route (track length x layer cost +
via cost + priced antipad web), of a strict route with the history off and all other copper in place, and of the same
route with only fixed copper in place, plus the nets that this free route meets. Run on v8 copper
(`runs/route-hand-d1.log`):

1. **Detours left in place (fixed by v9).** 47 nets had a strict route with the other copper in place that was
   shorter than their own route: /PHY_RBIAS 28.7 -> 19.1, /PHY_VDDCR 16.4 -> 12.4, /ETH_TXD0 60.3 -> 55.2, VBUS
   88.0 -> 86.4, /WDI 34.6 -> 32.9, ... Cause: the negotiation ends when the copper is legal. A net that went around
   another net (or around PathFinder history) keeps that route when the other net moves away, because a net with no
   conflict is not routed again (the v6 rule against swapping), and its route was searched with history and present
   cost, which have no meaning once the copper is legal. Nothing removed these detours.
2. **The RMII field is held by other routed nets (not fixed).** After fix 1, /ETH_TXD1, /ETH_TX_EN, /ETH_TXD0 cost
   65.4 / 63.1 / 55.2 against 22.3 / 20.5 / 21.4 with only fixed copper in place; their free routes meet /ETH_CRS_DV,
   /ETH_MDC, /ETH_MDIO, /PHY_REFCLK, /PHY_RSTn and the VDD_3V3 fan-out. /ETH_RXD1 39.4 vs 17.2 (/ETH_CRS_DV, /ETH_RXD0,
   /ETH_TX_EN), /UART0_RX 14.8 vs 8.7 (/UART0_TX), /PHY_VDDCR 12.4 vs 9.0 (/ETH_RXD1). A group re-route (the net
   first, then its non-plane blockers, all strict, kept only when the group objective falls) was built and measured:
   1 group kept (/PHY_LED1 /PHY_LED2 /J2_LEDG_A, -0.3) for 166 s of extra time, and the end length was 1564 mm (not
   better than fix 1 alone, 1559 mm). It was removed (not worth its time). This part is the negotiation order: in
   the v4 route the same field has /ETH_TXD0 19.5 mm.
3. **Antipad web price.** `--web-cost 0` (no fix 1): 1553.1 mm / 282 vias, but 14 signal via pairs with merged plane
   antipads against 2. The web price (3.0 per item, v6 fix 3c) keeps vias apart in the dense RMII field and costs
   about 40 mm. Not changed: the merged antipads are a real plane defect (v6 report), and no source gives a lower
   price.
4. **Model rules added after v4 (not a detour).** The v4 core (9ed2ed93) on today's model of the same placement:
   1539.2 mm / 283 vias with a GND hard fail ("no via site within 2 mm" at the U7 / U8 exposed pads, which v6 fixed
   with via-in-pad). VBUS: free route 85.4 against 86.4 now (v4 copper 68.3 mm): the per-layer current width and the
   rated pin escape of v8 (gate paths rules) make it longer; VDD_CORE likewise. The v4 copper is not a legal target
   under today's model, so 1495 mm is not a reachable bound for this model.
5. Other knobs measured, not changed (no source for a new value): `--hist 0.1` 1554.2 mm, not converged (1 net
   failed); `--via-cost 1` 1546.4 mm with 305 vias (acceptance limit 287).

## Fixes

| Fix | Change | Test (fails on v8 code) |

| 1 length recovery | `recover_length` after a legal end state (audit pairs 0): each routed signal / power net in the base order is routed again strictly (other copper blocks) with `Ctx::recover`: no history in the step, the via site or the stub price, and an admissible heuristic (octile distance to the nearest target box, grown by the longest escape stub; weight 1, so the A* without reopening returns the cheapest path). ## Determinism

Two runs of run.py, same input: ESP32-CORE (`v9/esp1`, `v9/esp2`) and the hand NET-CORE case (`v9/hand1`,
`v9/hand2`): the lock copy `input/*.kicad_pcb`, `route.json` and the routed board file are byte-identical (`cmp`).
`model.json` differs only in its `pcb` path field (the run folder). v8b had the same copper but a different via order
on ESP32-CORE because of the KIID order of the lock copy (fix 2).

## Remaining gaps

1. **Hand NET-CORE 1558.6 mm, not 1495 mm.** By cause: (a) RMII field, negotiation order: /ETH_TXD0 +16.4,
   /ETH_TXD1 +15.7, /ETH_TX_EN +3.4, /ETH_RXD1 +3.9, /ETH_REF_CLK +2.8, /ETH_MDIO +2.0 mm against v4. Each has a
   much shorter route when only fixed copper is in place, but the other RMII nets and the VDD_3V3 fan-out hold it.
   A strict group re-route did not find a legal and better state. Next: a short negotiation limited to the group
   (fresh history), kept only when the group objective falls. (b) /UART0_RX +5.4 (held by /UART0_TX), /PHY_VDDCR +2.6
   (held by /ETH_RXD1): the same cause, with one blocker. (c) VBUS +5.8 and VDD_CORE +2.1: even the free route is
   longer than the v4 copper, because of the per-layer current width and rated pin escape of v8 (model rules, not a
   detour). (d) Antipad web price: about 40 mm (diagnosis 3). v4 had none of the rules from v6 to v8; the v4 core on
   today's model gives 1539.2 mm with a GND hard fail.
2. **Recovery time.** One strict A* per net and round, with an admissible heuristic (more expansions than the
   1.15-weighted search) and a full pour check after each kept net: 405-484 s on H7-CORE / ESP32-CORE. Next: check
   only the pours on the layers of the changed copper, and give the placer judge (`compact_cli.py --router iotroute`)
   a `--recover 0` option where it needs speed.
3. The negotiation keeps its 1.15-weighted heuristic and its history. The recovery repairs the result afterwards; it
   does not change the convergence behaviour of v8, which every case needs.
4. Old gaps unchanged: isolation boards (DRU creepage), run.py parity on sub-sheets in subfolders, non-octilinear
   escape stubs.
