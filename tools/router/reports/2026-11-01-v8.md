## Regulator layout (datasheets in hardware-design/registry/datasheets/)

- TPS631000 (U3), SLVSFH3C section 7.4.1 (sha256 b52a4064...): input and output capacitors close to the IC, short
  traces, "wide and direct traces to the input and output capacitors", FB sense trace away from LX1 and LX2.
- TPS2116 (U2), SLVSFG1A section 10.1 (sha256 5babd88a...): all traces as short as possible, wide traces for VIN1, VIN2,
  VOUT and GND.

What the router follows: the full IPC width of the layer starts right after the pin escape (no neck past the pad
length; "wide close to the pins"); the A* takes the shortest path at that width (short, direct). Capacitor placement is
a placement item (unchanged, the committed placement). Not modelled: the FB-to-LX distance (no rule in the gate; see
gaps). On ESP32-CORE b6 the escapes of U2/U3 pass the paths gate (one rated piece each, full IPC width after it); the SW nets stay on F.Cu.

   modelled: the model stops (by design). Next: a class-pair clearance in the core (net class sets) and creepage as a

4. v7 small regressions (B.Cu GND pour pieces on NET-CORE 13 / 8, t1 VCC_1V8 neck 0.15 mm, H7 USB_VBUS 0.3 mm) are not
   changed: the NET-CORE copper of v8 is the v7 copper (metrics.py: same counts); no cause was worked on in this timebox.
5. Rule areas with a non-rectangular outline are kept as their bbox (conservative); pour keepouts are not in the pour
   model (noted per run).
7. run.py copies only the sheets next to the root sheet; BASE-PWR-C keeps sheets in subfolders (parity 19 on the copy).
6. The paths gate and the router agree on the pin escape only for pads in the gate's pin-ratings.json; an unrated
