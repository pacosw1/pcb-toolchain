# Length / skew tuning stage (`iotroute run --tune`): benchmark, 2026-11-06

Branch codex/router-tuning. Feature: tools/router/DESIGN.md 5f. Opt-in (policy `"tune": true`); default off.

## Boards and constraints

No active board (registry lifecycle `active`: ENV-SENSE, FIELD-24V-BUS, BASE-PWR, BASE-PWR-B, BASE-PWR-C, CELLULAR-B) has a
length or skew rule the tuning stage may act on:
- BASE-PWR, BASE-PWR-B, BASE-PWR-C: `skew (max 5mm)` on the USB pairs (`A.inDiffPair('/USB_D')`, `/USB_MCU_D`). These pairs are
  hand-routed copper that `iotroute run --lock-from` keeps locked; the policy never edits locked copper.
- BASE-PWR-C: `length (max 25mm)` on the MDI pairs: a max only (a meander only lengthens; the route already keeps DRU length max).
- ENV-SENSE, FIELD-24V-BUS, CELLULAR-B: no length or skew rule.

So the benchmark uses **fixture rules added only to the scratch copies** of two active boards (canonical board data unchanged):
- ENV-SENSE (development): `(rule "FIXTURE I2C skew" (condition "A.NetName == '/SDA' || A.NetName == '/SCL'") (constraint skew (max 2mm)))`.
  I2C does not need skew matching; the rule exists to give the stage a target on router-routed nets.
- FIELD-24V-BUS (holdout): `(rule "FIXTURE signal skew" (condition "A.NetName == '/Q1_GATE' || A.NetName == '/LED_A'") (constraint skew (max 1mm)))`.
  Chosen before seeing a tuned result; /LED_A is a ~1.45 mm net that needs ~9.5 mm more, which no legal meander of this stage holds.

Inputs (scratch project proj-tuning, boards from the bench-scratch copy of iot-lab origin/main; the iot-lab checkout was no longer on
disk when this ran): ENV-SENSE.kicad_pcb sha256 6abb00a0a30cd3f02d6e96a0cb326e16c1f050b735e5ed43770dcbf653844ae7, fixture
ENV-SENSE.kicad_dru be889ff49b22f453c0e00da60e9ccafcf28d9b38bb2d900bf35f2db18f2e95ef; FIELD-24V-BUS.kicad_pcb
sha256 pinned in the suite, fixture FIELD-24V-BUS.kicad_dru 813c2034af4ab29c6bc8622cbbd665e82545c894875d96020a7ec911699b6009.

`tools/pcb bench`, metric native, treatment router, both arms the same iotroute build, `tools/bench-in-route.sh` (route image

Per trial (iotroute record): every `off` trial fails exactly one DRC error, `skew_out_of_range` (ENV-SENSE skew 3.6-21.0 mm by the

FIELD-24V-BUS no legal meander fits /LED_A (a 1.45 mm net, 9.5 mm short), so `tune` = `off` there (same copper, same error). The gate
failures in the `tune` arm are the stale kicad-happy RP-001 exemption of /SCL (route-dependent: the routed /SCL has no via), not a
tuning artefact.

Not benchmarked: the skip of a correction round whose offsets are all under 0.01 mm (added after v2; it only saves the FIELD-24V-BUS
rerun, whose board was identical).

## What this does and does not show

- On a board where the short member has room (ENV-SENSE I2C fixture), the stage turns a skew failure into a native-clean board in
  8 of 8 seeds; KiCad's own lengths are needed for that (v1: 5 of 8 without them).
- The holdout shows the limit: a net with too little track for the needed length stays short (reported `met: false`).
- Not verified: real board constraints (none is tunable on active boards: their skew rules are on hand-locked pairs), router-routed
  pairs (codex/router-pairs), length-min rules on a real board, `diff_pair_uncoupled` with meanders, larger groups than two nets.
