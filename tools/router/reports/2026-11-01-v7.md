## Standoff cause (diagnosis first: `IOTROUTE_DIAG=<iteration>`)

The new debug output prints, for a small conflict set, each conflicting pair with the history under both items, then
for each involved net a strict route with the other copper in place and the single nets whose removal opens it.

1. **The history did not grow where the nets fought.** The audit added history on one grid cell per conflict sample
   (one via site per via). The losing net moved by one 0.05 mm pitch, stayed in conflict, and met cells with no
   history. Spread NET-CORE, iteration 50: the /PHY_RSTn via site had 0.3 of history after 50 iterations of conflict.
2. **Return vias are placed after the search.** A return via in conflict is charged to its owner, but the owner's A*
   never priced it. Spread NET-CORE: the /ETH_RXD0 return via sat on /ETH_CRS_DV while /ETH_RXD0 had a conflict-free
   route (strict route OK). H7-CORE (a2): two return vias (of /ETH_TXD0 and /LIMIT0_P) hole to hole, and a /PHY_LED2
   return on the only escape of /PHY_LED1.
3. **The same net always moved.** A net whose partner moved away keeps its copper (v6 rule against swapping), and the
   base order (power, fast, slow) gave the turn to the same net each time: H7 m3 VDD_3V3 moved 50 times between
   /SPI_SCK and /DAC2 + /PFAILn, which never moved; NET-CORE /ETH_TX_EN had no strict route until /PHY_RSTn moved.
4. **The open net needs one other net to move** (end step): the diagnosis found a single opener for every net with
   no strict route (/ETH_TX_EN: /ETH_TXD0 or /PHY_RSTn; /PHY_LED1: /PHY_LED2).

No hard rule (return-via reservation, antipad web, plane voids) blocked the alternatives: every listed net routed
strictly once one partner was removed.

## ESP32-CORE (unseen; main rev C; `run.py --lock-from` the committed board: the 3 diff pairs only, no via_count 0 nets)

**FAIL.** Binary a1 (fixes 1-3; same negotiation as a5 up to the end step): run.py verdict `FAIL 5 9 0` (KiCad DRC 5
errors, 9 unconnected, parity 0), 2067 tracks, 491 vias, 1785 s. Router fails (`esp/a1/.../result.json`, route.log):
/SW1_LX, /SW2_LX, VIN_REG, VSYS_IN "not reachable past fixed copper" from iteration 1 (hard fails, unchanged for 80
iterations), GND "no via site within 2 mm" of the pad at (151.25, 166.04). Cause: min_width 1.27 mm (2.1 A) against
the 0.28 mm pins of U2 at 0.5 mm pitch and the 1.6 mm L1 pads; the neck never goes below min_width (gap 1). Binary
a4 on ESP32-CORE: the negotiation was the same (5 hard fails), but the end step ran 17 min without a result (third-net
repair on walled nets and route_net on GND); stopped, fixed in a5 (05847970). a5 on ESP32-CORE: `FAIL 11 8 0`, 2071 tracks, 476 vias, 1284 s (KiCad DRC: 10 items_not_allowed, tracks of
/EXP_P02, /EXP_P06 on In2.Cu and /IO9, /U0TXD on B.Cu; 1 starved_thermal; a1 had 4 items_not_allowed + 1 starved_thermal).
The same 5 router fails; GND no via site near (151.25, 166.04) and (141.87, 134.43); the strict end step did not open
GND (cascade 7 open, restored). So the ESP32-CORE board has two input classes the router does not handle yet: power
pins narrower than the current width (gap 1) and the items_not_allowed areas (gap 7).

## Remaining gaps

1. **ESP32-CORE fails** (unseen check): 5 power nets cannot leave the regulator pins. Cause: the model gives them
   min_width 1.27 mm (2.1 A, IPC-2221 at dT 10 K, current-paths.json), and the pad-entry neck never goes below
   min_width, so no track enters a 0.28 mm pad at 0.5 mm pitch (U2). The paths gate gives a pin escape credit only from
   tools/pin-ratings.json; the router does not read it. Next: neck to the pin rating inside the pad-entry zone.
2. **B.Cu GND pour pieces** are more on both NET-CORE cases (13 vs 6, 8 vs 3; pieces under 10 mm2 9 vs 5, 6 vs 1). The
   count varies much between builds (t1: v5 12, v6 3) and no gate checks it. H7-CORE is at v6 level (70 vs 74).
3. **Power width:** t1 VCC_1V8 necks to 0.15 mm (v6 0.3), H7 USB_VBUS 0.3 mm for 5.3 mm (v6 0); the paths gate passes.
4. **Fix 2 (rip order) and the via count:** run a1 (fixes 1-3 only) put 633 vias on H7-CORE; fix 5 brought it to 556.
   The interaction of the rip order with the via count is not measured further.
5. **Speed:** the priced web costs less (near_in 66 % -> 49 % of the samples), but fix 5 (more searches per rejected
   layer change) costs time; wall times here were measured with the Mac at load 14-23 (another agent), so they are not
   a clean comparison with v6.
6. `IOTROUTE_DIAG` runs strict routes per iteration: debug only (slow).
7. **items_not_allowed** (ESP32-CORE): KiCad reports router tracks on In2.Cu / B.Cu inside areas where they are not
   allowed; the model does not give these areas to the router. Not examined further in this timebox.
8. The third-net repair on a plane net (GND on ESP32-CORE) tries every net with copper near any GND pad: slow on a
   failing board (a5 end step about 9 min).
